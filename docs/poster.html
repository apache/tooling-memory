Durable memory for disposable agents, on one page

Agents start empty and end after one task. What they learn, decide and owe lives in a shared record that people own. This page reads the design as three loops through its five layers: work, trust and improvement. Read the diagram from the top; the paper is durable-memory-for-disposable-agents.md.
