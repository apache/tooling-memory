Durable memory for disposable agents, on one page

Agents start empty and end after one task. What they learn, decide and owe lives in a shared record that people own. Three loops run through five layers: work, trust, and improvement. Read the diagram from the top; the paper is durable-memory-for-disposable-agents.md.

Where colour appears

A person decides. Promotion to verified, a new rule above the ceiling, an exception, an irreversible action, each move of content to a lower label. Everything an agent does is inside limits a person set.

Refused. A call over its destination's ceiling does not leave. Below the ceiling is the normal case and has no colour.

The word under each part names the premise it serves: disposable agents, untrusted output from models, ownership that limits conflict, viability over years, evaluation built in, and data that outlives its parts. Appendix H sorts every part, rule and failure mode under those six.
